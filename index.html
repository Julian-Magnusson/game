HOW TO FLY

Controls

Course rules

Follow the glowing line and fly through the gates in order. Altitude drains as you fly. Lime boost rings and light beams lift you.

A missed gate adds 2 seconds. A crash adds 3 seconds and returns you to your last gate. Flying far off course warns you, then returns you with an extra 5 seconds.

Difficulty

  • Easy: open avenues and regular rings; no side streets or course obstacles.
  • Medium: a few wider side streets and obstacles; regular rings.
  • Hard: more narrow turns and obstacles; smaller rings.
  • Extreme: narrow streets throughout, smaller rings and faster flight.

Screen orientation

The game requests a fixed landscape side. If the browser rotates anyway, the game view turns back to the side used at Fly. Browser bars may still rotate.

To stop the screen rotating at all, turn on the phone's rotation lock, open the game in portrait and tap play sideways. The game then turns itself to the side you hold the phone.

Courses and times

Copy a course code or share the page URL to fly the same course and difficulty again. Your best time is saved in this browser. Medals use your flight time plus penalties. Earn Gold or Diamond on Hard to unlock Extreme for that course.

Diamond matches the clean reference run with no extra time allowance. Keep every gate, cut corners where there is room and use clean dives to gain speed.
