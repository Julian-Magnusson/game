# Neon Flight

Play at **https://julian-magnusson.github.io/game/**.

Fly through a generated city with mouse/keyboard on desktop, or phone tilt and an optional
touch stick on mobile. Collect permanent stars shared across difficulties and spend them in Shop.

A **Collapse Key** costs **10 stars** and permanently reveals faint red rings on **Hard and
Extreme**. Finding one starts an escape through falling buildings and burning gardens. Reach
the sewer shaft to unlock **The Sewer** menu and its original song **Undertow**. The menu's
manhole button takes you underground and back to the surface. Underground courses are coming
in a later release.

This repository contains the published single-file build, `index.html`. Development sources,
tests and the project plan live in the separate `game-demo` workspace.
